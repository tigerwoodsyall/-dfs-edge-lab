Daily Injury & Rotation Planner · All Teams

Role-aware minutes use recent starter/bench observations, similar teammate absences where at least two examples exist, and recency-weighted workloads. Blowouts and possible foul trouble are downweighted; reported early exits receive little weight. No injury or ejection cause is invented when source data is unavailable. The model starts with up to ten rotation players when role data is available and can enable extra eligible bench players if needed to fill the court-minute budget. Explicit inclusions and fixed minutes take priority. Minutes ranges describe workload uncertainty and are not calibrated probability intervals.

Every loaded slate team is reconciled to 240 regulation minutes and 48 at each court position. Observed minutes are normalized to regulation game length, then the available rotation is balanced within the same team budget. Player-specific workload ranges and a penalty for large changes prevent the solver from routinely assigning 40–42 minutes. Injury replacements can receive larger increases; fixed manual minutes remain available.

Daily roster positions cover all 30 NBA teams. Primary roles use roster positions and DK-position proxies; secondary roles and depth are inferred, not confirmed coaching decisions. Players with no current-season appearances default out of the projected rotation unless explicitly included or reported Available. Questionable/Doubtful players stay in unless you change the assumption. Usage and per-minute rates are not automatically boosted by unverified on/off effects.

DFS Slate Foundry Domination · Base Model

A transparent statistical baseline built from completed regular-season box scores. Current-season usage and fantasy production per minute drive the baseline, with historical production providing a smaller stabilizing sample. Set projected minutes and usage below. Estimates are not fitted or backtested yet.

Model weights

Default production and usage blend: 80% current season, 20% historical. Weights normalize across available samples; a missing current-season sample falls back to history. Expand Stat forecast to see the observed current-season DK points per minute and usage. Auto minutes blend historical regulation-equivalent averages with a current-season prior (65% median of the last 5 played games, 35% season mean). Completed-game minutes are scaled to a 240-minute team total so overtime does not inflate the baseline. Player-specific auto caps and stronger protection of established core roles guide team balancing; 42 minutes is no longer a generic starter cap. Blank minute/usage overrides use the auto estimates shown. Usage is a box-score estimate of possessions used while on court. Usage overrides scale points, made threes, assists and turnovers; rebounds, steals and blocks retain their per-minute rates. DraftKings bonuses use the weighted observed bonus rate. Upside is an uncalibrated normal approximation, not a proven 95th percentile. The Rotations tab reconciles all slate teams to 240 minutes and redistributes official injury absences. Court-role assumptions are editable; usage changes remain manual unless supported by the observed inputs.

Early-season shooting adjustment

For fewer than 20 current-season games, compare 2PT, 3PT and FT percentages with last season’s regular-season sample. Material differences (5, 7 and 8 percentage points) receive an attempt-weighted adjustment that fades with more games and attempts. Current shot volume, minutes and usage remain in place. At least 10 historical games and 200 minutes are required; missing shooting data produces no adjustment. The change is capped at 20% of projected scoring points, and rebounds, assists, steals and blocks are unchanged. Expand Stat forecast to see the adjustment. These settings are model assumptions, not backtested calibration.

Estimated field ownership

Roster-aware popularity estimate: projected points plus a value bonus (3 points per standardized points-per-$1,000 advantage, capped at ±3 standard deviations), 8% of projected upside, and a modest discount for known low minutes. Value is relative to the whole slate: discounted stars can rate highly alongside cheap players. The estimate fits eight DK slots, at most 100% ownership per player, and an implied average salary no greater than $50,000. Strong value plays provide salary relief for expensive stars. Flex eligibility is normalized so alternate slots do not count as extra copies of a player. Concentration controls how heavily ownership favors stronger choices. Imported/manual values are protected, including explicit zeros. Apply Ownership Only uses the current Projections inputs; applying the full Base Model uses its own projection preview. This is a marginal field estimate, not a simulated field or an ownership model fitted to past contest outcomes.

Review the blended projections

Weight 1 gives sites equal influence; weight 2 gives a site double influence. A weight of 0 or an unchecked source excludes it. Each player’s average uses only sites with a valid projection for that player. Two or more included sites projecting 0 flag a player as likely out and override the blend to 0, even when other sites are positive or the minimum source count is higher. Any active imported source with a zero or missing projection forces projection and ownership to zero. Sources with conflicting duplicate player rows exclude that player until corrected.
